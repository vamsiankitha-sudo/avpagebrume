Kindness Day is 13 November, a few days after Diwali. Max, Coco, Freya, and Bruno are the helpers in the stories below.

What are some World Kindness Day activities for kids?

World Kindness Day is November 13. Kids can make a card for someone, share something they have, welcome a new friend, help take care of the neighborhood, and draw a kind act of their own. These five pages give each activity a short story to read aloud and a Let's Talk question.
